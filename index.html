Red is warmer than the baseline you chose, blue is cooler, pale is barely changed. The scale is symmetric, so +1 °C and −1 °C are the same distance from the middle.

An anomaly is a difference, not a temperature. It says how far a place sits from its own average, so cold places and warm places can be compared on the same scale.

Grey cells have no data. Before about 1950 the gaps are large and real — nobody was measuring there. A gap is not a zero.

Hover or tap a cell for its own record. Everything works from the keyboard: tab to the controls, arrow keys on the map, space to play or pause.
